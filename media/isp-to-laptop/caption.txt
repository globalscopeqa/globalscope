How does the internet actually reach your desk? 🌐➡️💻

Every web page you open takes a 6-step journey in milliseconds:
1️⃣ ISP fibre: light pulses arrive at the demarc, where your network begins
2️⃣ Router + firewall: every packet inspected, threats blocked
3️⃣ Core switch: the backbone, moving data at 10 Gb/s+
4️⃣ Access switches + PoE: data and power to phones, cameras and APs
5️⃣ Wi-Fi: secure WPA3 connection
6️⃣ Your laptop ✅

LINKEDIN
--------
Your team clicks a link. The page loads in 12 ms. Behind that click sits a six-stage journey most people never see:

→ ISP fibre lands at the demarcation point, where the provider's network ends and yours begins
→ The edge router and firewall inspect every packet and stop threats before they reach a user
→ The core switch moves traffic between floors and servers at 10 Gb/s and up
→ Access switches carry data, and PoE power, to phones, cameras and access points
→ Wi-Fi 6E with WPA3-Enterprise connects devices securely
→ The laptop
