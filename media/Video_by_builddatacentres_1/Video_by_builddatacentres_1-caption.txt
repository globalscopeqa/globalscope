How do you design the electrical infrastructure for a 200 MW AI data centre? ⚡

At this scale it's not just picking transformers. It's a utility-scale power system, from the grid connection all the way to the GPU rack:
1️⃣ Grid interconnection and an HV receiving substation
2️⃣ Independent A/B power paths
3️⃣ HV/MV transformers with fault-current control
4️⃣ Sectionalised MV distribution, so a fault stays small
5️⃣ UPS and critical power to bridge any outage
6️⃣ MV generators with black-start and staged load restoration
7️⃣ SCADA/EPMS monitoring, plus room to grow without touching live loads

The golden rule: never let 200 MW behave like one electrical fault domain. 🛡️

Utility lost? UPS bridges → generators start → synchronise → cooling stabilises → load blocks restore. ⚡

GRID → HV SUBSTATION → TRANSFORMER → MV → UPS → BUSWAY → GPU
