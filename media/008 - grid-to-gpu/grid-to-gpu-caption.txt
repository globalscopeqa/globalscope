How does power actually reach a GPU? ⚡➡️🖥️

A GPU may be the final destination, but the journey starts kilometres away:
1️⃣ Grid: high-voltage utility supply arrives at the site
2️⃣ MV switchgear: metering, protection and fault isolation
3️⃣ Transformer: steps voltage down for the facility
4️⃣ LV distribution: power routed to critical loads
5️⃣ UPS: conditions power and bridges outages until generators take over
6️⃣ Busway + rack PDUs: power delivered to every rack
7️⃣ Server PSU + VRMs: converted to the exact low voltage the GPU needs ✅

From grid to GPU, every stage transforms, protects or distributes power. Get one wrong and the whole rack goes dark.
